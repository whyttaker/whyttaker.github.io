About

Computer Science student at UC Irvine's Donald Bren School of ICS, with a minor in Game Design. Currently a Software Development Engineer at Amazon, where I build and maintain scalable distributed systems and AI-powered tooling for financial infrastructure handling hundreds of millions of daily transactions.

I'm drawn to the intersection of engineering scale and creative craft — whether that's architecting event-driven pipelines on AWS, leading a game team to win a design competition, or pushing the limits of what LLMs can do in production. I've worked across cloud infrastructure, full-stack development, machine learning, and game development, and I find the most interesting problems tend to live where those disciplines overlap.

Outside of engineering, I competed as a Division I athlete — an experience that shaped how I think about team dynamics, composure under pressure, and showing up consistently. Dean's List, Scholar-Athlete, and 1st Place at the UC Irvine Video Game Design Competition.
