<section data-block="overview">
<span class="t-mono">Overview</span>
## About the Project

H & J Cabinets is a custom cabinetry business, and their previous site undersold the craftsmanship of the work. I took on a freelance contract to redesign the site from the ground up: a fresh visual identity built around hundreds of photos and videos of finished installs, paired with an SEO pass to get that work actually found. Since launch, organic traffic is up 23%, and the business has seen roughly 10x more lead calls over the past year compared to prior years.
</section>

<section data-block="process">
<span class="t-mono">Design Process</span>
## Design & Vision

Custom cabinetry sells on craftsmanship, and craftsmanship reads through photos and video, not copy. The redesign was built around that: a media-first structure that gives the hundreds of project photos and videos room to actually show the work, rather than the site's own template getting in the way of it.
</section>

<section data-block="implementation">
<span class="t-mono">Implementation</span>
## Redesign & SEO

The rebuild covered the full site: new visual design, navigation, and a media library restructured to handle a much larger volume of photo and video content without slowing the site down. Alongside the visual redesign, I ran an SEO pass across the site's structure, on-page content, and metadata so the work now surfaced in the searches that actually lead to a hired job.
</section>

<section data-block="challenges">
<span class="t-mono">Key Engineering Challenges</span>
## What Was Hard

The core tension was volume versus speed: hundreds of photos and videos are what sell the craftsmanship, but naively dropping that much media into a site tanks load times and, with it, the SEO gains the redesign was supposed to deliver. Getting both (a rich, media-heavy portfolio and a fast, well-ranked site) took real attention to how that media was organized and served.
</section>

<section data-block="takeaways">
<span class="t-mono">Takeaways</span>
## What I Learned

This project was a good reminder that for a business like this, the website's job is to get out of the way of the work. The redesign and the SEO pass only mattered because the results were measurable: a 23% lift in organic traffic and roughly 10x more lead calls aren't abstract wins, they're the actual thing a small business needed from its site.
</section>
